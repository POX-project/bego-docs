# ROM Mirror Gallery

Welcome to the Begonia ROM Mirror! Select your preferred ROM below to download the latest stable builds.

> [!WARNING]
> Because Begonia uses a MediaTek chipset, flashing ROMs carries a higher risk of soft-bricking if you flash an incorrect base. Always ensure you are on the correct firmware (usually MIUI 12.5 / A11) before flashing!
