# ROMs Store

Welcome to the **ROMs Store** for the Redmi Note 8 Pro (Begonia)! Here is a comprehensive list of the most popular and actively maintained custom ROMs available for our device.

> [!WARNING]
> Because Begonia uses a MediaTek chipset, flashing ROMs carries a higher risk of soft-bricking if you flash an incorrect base. Always ensure you have a fallback unbrick method (like SP Flash Tool) and that you are on the correct firmware (usually MIUI 12.5 / A11) before flashing!

## Google Pixel Experience

If you want your Begonia to look and feel exactly like a Google Pixel phone, these are the best options:

- **[PixelExperience](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  The gold standard for the Pixel look. It includes all Pixel goodies (launcher, wallpapers, icons, fonts, and boot animation) out of the box. Highly stable for daily driving.
  
- **[PixelOS](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  Similar to PixelExperience but often updated with additional under-the-hood performance tweaks.
  
- **[Evolution X](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  A Pixel-like ROM but heavily injected with customization features and gaming optimizations. Perfect if you want Pixel aesthetics but need more control over your UI.

## Performance & Customization

If your primary goal is gaming, performance, or tweaking every single aspect of the UI:

- **[crDroid](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  Based on LineageOS but packed with an insane amount of customization options. It is widely considered one of the best ROMs for Begonia due to its perfect balance of stability, battery life, and gaming performance.
  
- **[Corvus OS](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  A ROM built specifically for gamers. It strips out unnecessary bloat and focuses entirely on CPU/GPU performance.
  
- **[Nusantara Project](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  An Indonesian ROM known for its beautiful custom UI elements and very smooth performance.

## Clean & Minimal

If you want a bloat-free, minimalist experience focused on privacy and battery life:

- **[LineageOS](https://xdaforums.com/f/redmi-note-8-pro-roms-kernels-recoveries-ot.9395/)**  
  The classic custom ROM. It offers a pure, near-stock Android experience. Extremely stable and the best choice for maximizing battery life.
  
- **[/e/OS](https://e.foundation/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/e-logo.png)**  
  A fully "de-Googled" privacy-focused ROM. It removes all Google services and telemetry, offering a completely private environment.
