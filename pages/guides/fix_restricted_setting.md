# Fix Restricted Settings Warning

On Android 13+ (and newer HyperOS/MIUI ports), side-loading applications that require Accessibility or Notification access (like MacroDroid or AutoClickers) will result in a greyed-out "Restricted Setting" warning.

## How to bypass
You don't need root to bypass this!

1. Go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Manage Apps**.
2. Find the app you just side-loaded.
3. Tap the **3-dot menu** in the top right corner.
4. Select **Allow restricted settings**.
5. Authenticate with your fingerprint or PIN.
6. You can now grant the app the required Accessibility permissions.
