Unlock Bootloader & Install TWRP

Follow these steps carefully to unlock your bootloader and flash the Team Win Recovery Project (TWRP).

Data Wiping Warning
Unlocking the bootloader will completely erase all data on your device. Ensure you have backed up all important photos, contacts, and messages.

1. Unlocking the Bootloader

  1. Enable Developer Options by tapping "MIUI Version" in Settings > About Phone 7 times.
  2. Go to Additional Settings > Developer Options and enable OEM Unlocking and USB Debugging.
  3. Bind your Xiaomi account in Mi Unlock Status.
  4. Download the official Mi Unlock Tool on your PC.
  5. Boot your phone into Fastboot mode (Volume Down + Power).
  6. Connect your phone to the PC and run the Mi Unlock Tool. Follow the on-screen instructions.

2. Flashing TWRP

Once your bootloader is unlocked, you can flash a custom recovery.

Required Files

  • The latest TWRP or CFW recovery image for Begonia (e.g., recovery.img).
  • Begonia Vbmeta image (required to bypass verified boot).

Fastboot Commands

Open a command prompt in your Platform Tools folder and execute the following commands:

fastboot --disable-verity --disable-verification flash vbmeta vbmeta.img
fastboot flash recovery recovery.img
fastboot boot recovery.img

Your phone should now boot directly into TWRP.
